# Privacy Policy – Calm Tab Groups

_Last updated: November 23, 2026_

Calm Tab Groups works entirely on your own computer.

**What the extension accesses**
- The web addresses (URLs) of your open tabs. They are used only to decide which tab
  group a tab belongs to, at the moment of sorting. They are not stored, logged or sent anywhere.
- The tab groups in your browser, in order to create, name and color them.

**What the extension stores**
- Your group settings (group names, colors and the website patterns you entered).
  They are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`, which means Google Chrome syncs them between
  the computers where you are signed in to Chrome, if you use Chrome Sync. Nothing else is stored.

**What the extension does not do**
- It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party.
- It makes no network requests at all.
- It contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no remote code.
- It does not sell or transfer user data, and it does not use data for purposes unrelated
  to sorting your tabs.

**Removing your data**
Uninstalling the extension removes all of its stored settings. You can also delete all
groups at any time under *Settings → Start over*.

**Contact**
Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/Surferdbeere/calm-tab-groups/issues.

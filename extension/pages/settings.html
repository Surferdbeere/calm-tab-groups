Backup & import

Save your groups to a file, or load them from one. Importing replaces your current groups. Files exported from the “Auto Group Tabs” extension work too.

Your settings are synced with your Chrome profile, so they follow you to other computers where you are signed in.

Start over

Run the setup assistant again, or remove all groups.

Privacy

Calm Tab Groups works entirely on your computer. It reads the addresses of your tabs only to decide which group they belong to. Nothing is sent anywhere, there are no analytics and no tracking.
